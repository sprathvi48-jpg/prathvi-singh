Reuse: Use items again when safe.

Recycle: Send accepted materials to the right recycling stream.

Quick bin guide

🟢 Wet / organic: food scraps and garden waste for suitable composting.

🔵 Dry recyclables: clean paper, metal, glass and accepted plastics.

🟠 Special handling: batteries, e-waste, chemicals and sanitary waste—keep separate and use approved local routes.
